Who We Are

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Persatuan Pengurusan Teknologi (MOTIC) is the premier academic body for Technology Management students, established under the School of Technology Management and Logistics. Our ongoing purpose is to enhance the academic experience and future readiness of our students.

Vision

To be recognised as the leading academic association in UUM, consistently enhancing the marketability and success of students majoring in Technology Management.

Mission

  • To champion the welfare and holistic development of all STML students, with a dedicated focus on those in Technology Management.
  • To continuously innovate and improve the academic activities available to Technology Management students.
  • To strengthen the essential soft skills of our students through impactful, well-organised association activities.

Our Objectives

  • To facilitate the ongoing exchange of contemporary knowledge and industry insights for students of Technology Management.
  • To promote and support the dynamic activities and programmes led by our students.
  • To provide current guidance on relevant subjects and emerging fields of focus for study and research.
